# Personality and microcopy

## Personality

Decide it **before** choosing type, color and imagery, and keep every signal pointing the same way.

| Signal | Playful | Neutral | Serious / formal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Color | Saturated, vibrant | Simple | Muted, low saturation |
| Typeface | Rounded sans | Plain sans | Serif / elegant |
| Radius (buttons, cards) | Large | Medium (~8) | Small / 0 |
| Icon roundness | Rounded | Either | Sharp |
| Imagery | Colorful illustrations | Simple assets | Real photos |
| Language | "Hey there! Wanna join us?" | Clear and plain | "Welcome. We're an exclusive community…" |
| Fits | Kids, young audiences | Anything (the safest choice) | Banks, luxury, trust |

**Don't mix signals:** a serif face with a candy palette and cartoon illustrations looks wrong. Don't copy another brand's personality, because it rests on their values, not yours.

## Microcopy

You own the text in buttons, dialogs, errors and empty states.

### Button labels

Use a verb plus an object, short and specific.

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| "Next" | "Next step" |
| "Favorite" | "Add to favorites" |
| "Add this product to favorites" (too long) | "Add to favorites" |

### Confirmation dialogs

- **Name the object and the exact consequence.** Avoid ambiguous pairs like "Cancel" / "Yes".
- **Example:**
  - Title: "Remove file"
  - Body: "The file "IMG_0123.jpg" will be permanently removed."
  - Buttons: "No, keep it" / "Yes, remove this file"
- **For a subscription:** "Close" / "Cancel subscription".

### Errors

Say what happened and how to fix it. "No internet connection. Try restarting your router." is better than "No wireless connection found."

### Tone

- **Sound human:** simple words and contractions ("don't", "that's"). Drop the contractions only for very formal products.
- **Keep it short.** Avoid jargon unless the audience is experts.
- **No Lorem Ipsum.** Write realistic text about the product. For long articles, use a real-looking sample.
