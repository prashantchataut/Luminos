## 2. Rules quoted and how each was applied

> Preserve the product's identity, reuse existing owners, and prefer no finding to an unsupported one.

*Applied:* Kept the Luminos data layer (locker, theme tokens, skin catalog) and replaced only the screens; each round critique below cites a rendered defect, not a taste.

**improve-ui** (`.agents/skills/improve-ui/SKILL.md`)
> Never modify product source. Create or edit files only under `design-plans/`.

*Applied:* This skill is read-only by design. It was used as an audit lens only; all edits were made by this session's own workflow, not by the skill.

**better-layout** (`.agents/skills/better-layout/SKILL.md`)
> Space groups first, background shapes second, separator lines last and only where space alone can't carry the structure.

*Applied:* Zero wireframe borders: rows and cards are filled surfaces, 20-28px gaps between groups, no divider lines.

**better-layout** (`.agents/skills/better-layout/SKILL.md`)
> Size text containers with `max-width` and `min-height`, never a fixed width or height, and let rows wrap.

*Applied:* Preference rows use `min-height: 68px` and wrap long copy. Mod and cosmetic cards keep the fixed 160px / 215px heights the brief specifies, with truncating labels.

**better-layout** (`.agents/skills/better-layout/SKILL.md`)
> Give each view one primary action.

*Applied:* Home has one filled Launch Game button; version and Host World are quiet secondaries.

**better-motion** (`.agents/skills/better-motion/SKILL.md`)
> Keystrokes, row hovers and the content behind a tab change instantly, or with a transition of `150ms` or less on `opacity` or `background-color`.

*Applied:* Screen change is a 120ms opacity fade; card and nav hovers are 140ms background/colour transitions.

**better-motion** (`.agents/skills/better-motion/SKILL.md`)
> Modals, drawers and panels | `350ms`–`450ms`

*Applied:* The module settings drawer slides in at 350ms on `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`; modals take 250ms.

**taste-skill** (`.agents/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md`)
> **Equal left and right content edges.**

*Applied:* Every page sits in one centred 1200px column with equal 40px side padding (`components/Page.tsx`).

**taste-skill** (`.agents/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md`)
> **One system:**

*Applied:* One button pair (`btn-primary`, `btn-quiet`), one card surface, one radius scale, one pill style across all screens.

**audit-ai-design-slop** (`.agents/skills/audit-ai-design-slop/SKILL.md`)
> Do not guess whether AI made the design.

*Applied:* The critiques cite concrete defects (squashed buttons, clipped art), never origin.
